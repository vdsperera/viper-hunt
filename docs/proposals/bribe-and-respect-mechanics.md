# Proposed Gameplay Mechanics: Bribe & Direct Destroy

**Status:** `[Not Implemented]`
**Date Logged:** 2026-09-07

## 1. Bribing the Police
**Description:**
The player can bribe the police to reduce the number of police officers actively pursuing the hunter.
* **Cost/Trade-off:** Using this feature will cost the player resources and will decrease their "Lawful" alignment or rule-following criteria.

**Gameplay Impact:**
* Provides a great risk/reward tactical choice. It gives players an emergency escape valve if they are overwhelmed.
* Balances the game by attaching a long-term consequence (lower Lawful stat) to an immediate benefit.
* Encourages diverse playstyles (e.g., a wealthy, corrupt hunter vs. a strict, rule-abiding hunter).

## 2. Direct Target Destruction (No Hand-off)
**Description:**
When dealing with a target, the hunter has the choice to directly destroy/execute them instead of the standard "hand-off" to the police.
* **Cost/Trade-off:** Doing this increases a "Respect" or "Underworld Fear" criteria, but significantly decreases the "Lawful" criteria.

**Gameplay Impact:**
* Introduces a moral dilemma and alignment system. Handing off to the police is the "safe" and legal route, while taking matters into your own hands builds reputation but violates the law.
* Paves the way for branching paths or faction allegiances. High "Respect" could unlock underworld perks or black market items, whereas high "Lawful" could unlock police support or official gear.
